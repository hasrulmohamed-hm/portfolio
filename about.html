//Trajectory

10 years of data pipelines,
cloud warehouses & applied AI.

I began my engineering journey in Computer Science (Systems & Networking) at Universiti Tenaga Nasional, mastering distributed systems, networks, and database engines. Over the last decade, I have engineered data architectures across legacy migrations, nationwide financial guarantee platforms, and enterprise data platforms at PETRONAS. My driving standard is clear: eliminate manual bottlenecks, automate schema validation and QA/QC, and build resilient, production-ready data pipelines.

01 /Principles

How I approach data & engineering.

01 · Radical Automation

14 Days to 30 Minutes

Manual data handling is technical debt waiting to happen. Through automated schema validation, execution plan tuning, and batch optimization, repetitive multi-week integrations become deterministic sub-hour pipelines.

02 · Pre-Load QA/QC Integrity

Zero-Defect Data Quality

Garbage in, garbage out ruins analytics and ML models. Enforcing rigorous pre-load validation, automated data patching, and field-level confidence scoring ensures downstream trust across all stakeholders.

03 · Practical Applied AI

AI with Traceability

Integrating computer vision, Azure AI Form Recognizer, and OCR into data pipelines should solve tangible bottlenecks. Extracted data must be normalized, auditable, and traceable back to source documents.
